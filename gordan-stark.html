Why Beast Mastery

From Icy Veins’ Forever guides (updated Sep 30, 2026): hunters are the strongest leveling class, and Beast Mastery doubles down on that strength.

Beast Mastery Pet is your tank and a huge share of damage. Feign Death, ranged pressure, and Summon Hawk at 25. Top-tier solo leveling.
Marksmanship Still excellent, but invests in your shots over the pet. Lone Wolf / Trueshot 21-point path is dungeon-oriented, not open-world leveling.
Survival Weakest leveling pick until deep in the tree (~40+). Early points are meek; you still play ranged for a long stretch.
Professions Icy Veins calls Skinning + Leatherworking one of the strongest natural hunter pairs — kill, send the pet to the next pull, skin, craft.

Stats & mana

Out of mana? Keep Auto Shotting — most power is basics + pet. Mend Pet and Serpent Sting are efficient mana spends.

Stat priority while leveling

Weapon damage (prefer slower, harder-hitting) → Hit → Agility → Crit → Intellect (with Careful Aim later) → Spirit (leveling regen) → Stamina last.

Beast Mastery tree (Forever)

One point per level from 10. Gates at 0 / 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 points already spent in BM. Summon Hawk is the 16-point gold medal (level 25 earliest if every point stayed in BM). Bestial Wrath needs Intimidation and 30 BM points already spent. Tree holds 44 points; at 60 you have 51.

Row 1 · gate 0

  • Deadly Aspects 5 — Hawk haste proc; also melee haste while Aspect of the Beast.
  • Endurance Training 5 — Pet health and armor.

Row 2 · gate 5

  • Focused Fire 2 — Damage you and pet deal while pet is active.
  • Improved Aspect of the Monkey 3 — More dodge; pet gains half.
  • Pathfinding 2 — Cheetah / Pack speed.
  • Improved Revive Pet 2 — Faster, cheaper, healthier revive.

Row 3 · gate 10

  • Bestial Swiftness 1 — Pet moves 30% faster. Required for Intimidation.
  • Unleashed Fury 5 — Pet and hawk damage.

Row 4 · gate 15

  • Improved Mend Pet 2 — Cleanse chance and mana cut.
  • Ferocity 5 — Pet and hawk crit.
  • Summon Hawk 1 — Instant dive, hawk for 18s, max 2; shares CD with Arcane Shot.

Row 5 · gate 20

  • Spirit Bond 2 — You and pet regen health.
  • Intimidation 1 — Pet stun + 100% crit on that attack. Needs Bestial Swiftness.
  • Bestial Discipline 2 — Pet focus regen; mana regen while casting.

Row 6 · gate 25

  • Frenzy 5 — Pet attack-speed after crits. Needs Ferocity 5.

Row 7 · gate 30

  • Bestial Wrath 1 — Pet rage, +50% damage 18s. Needs Intimidation.

What can still change

  • Buy vs skip tags are a recommendation for solo casual Beast Mastery (Normal), not a Blizzard rule. Trainer prices cited from UrGameTips Forever spellbook are base prices before reputation discounts; Aimed Shot ranks 2+ omit price because the listed figure looked unreliable.
  • Class abilities and talents can still change through the beta. The deep dive says so.
  • Early drafts used the Oct 1, 2026 beta level-30 cap. Launch is live; rows from 31–60 were filled from Forever database / projected BM spends and should be rechecked in-game.
  • No full Beast Mastery point path is published. The talent field on every level is blank on purpose.
  • Parry at 8, Dual Wield at 20, and Mail at 40 are Classic trainer skills. They are missing from the Forever hunter ability list, so they stay classic-unconfirmed.
  • Growl ranks 1 and 2 are not on the Forever ability list. Ranks 3 through 7 are, at 20, 30, 40, 50, and 60. Do not assume rank 1 is learned at pet level 1.
  • Bite, Claw, Dash, and Dive ranks are learned from beasts, not bought as a level-up spell. Their Classic pet levels are not restated as Forever ranks here.
  • Family abilities such as Swipe, Mine!, and Dust Cloud appear on Wowhead at required level 1. That is the spell's floor, not a hunter level-1 training visit. Use the deep dive for what each family does, not a level.
  • Aspect of the Hawk rank 6 (level 58) lists 55 ranged attack power after rank 5 listed 90. Hunter's Mark rank 4 lists 71 after rank 3 listed 98. Reported as the tooltips read. They may be bad data.
  • Leatherworking recipe skill ranks for the Trapper pieces are not on the spell pages. Only the wear level and reagents are.
  • Skinning leather types by character level (ruined, light, medium, and so on) are not taken from a Forever source, so they are omitted.
  • Human Hunter starts in Northshire Valley, Elwynn Forest (ForeverDB Human Hunter guide, updated 2026-11-08; trainers seen in Forever: Tordrin Sternblade in Northshire, Josephine Carson in Goldshire, Einris Brightspear in Stormwind). Skinning in Elwynn: Helene Peltskinner (beta notes / prior Forever skinning note). This guide is for the Normal ruleset (PvE), not Roleplaying.
  • Season of Discovery spells that share the hunter filter (Chimera Shot, Kill Command, Carve, rune engravings, Aspect of the Viper, Aspect of the Falcon) are excluded. They are not in the hunter deep dive.
  • Summon Hawk, Sniper Shot, Trueshot Aura, and Counterattack have trainable ranks but are talents, not spells every hunter learns. They are not in the level spell lists. Counterattack is Survival. Sniper Shot and Trueshot Aura are Marksmanship.
  • Respec cost of 1 silver is a temporary beta rule, not a launch rule.
  • Forever launched November 4, 2026. Early rows still cite beta-era ability lists; cross-check Icy Veins Forever BM (Nov 4, 2026) and ForeverDB when a tooltip disagrees.
  • Icy Veins BM leveling coverage stops at the beta level-30 cap. Rows 31–60 on this page are a projected spend using real Forever BM (and late Survival) talents — not an Icy Veins list.
