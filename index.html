                                                                                                        Rotation

                                                                                                        1. Aspect of the Hawk up for fights. Aspect of the Cheetah while traveling (taking damage dazes you and drops it).
                                                                                                        2. Hunter's Mark on the target before the pull.
                                                                                                        3. Send your pet in first, abilities on auto-cast.
                                                                                                        4. Once the pet has hit it: Serpent Sting.
                                                                                                        5. Summon Hawk on cooldown, keeping 2 hawks up.
                                                                                                        6. Aimed Shot on cooldown. Multi-Shot instead against several mobs.
                                                                                                        7. Auto Shot the whole time.
                                                                                                        8. Pulled aggro off the pet? Disengage.
                                                                                                        9. Arcane Shot only when you're near full mana and 2 hawks will stay up for 6+ more seconds.

                                                                                                        Out of mana? Keep Auto Shot and pet going. Spend mana on Mend Pet and Serpent Sting first.

                                                                                                        Source: Icy Veins Forever BM.

                                                                                                        Pet handling

                                                                                                        • Let the pet build threat before you open. If you pull aggro, wait longer before shooting next time. Staying at range is faster than meleeing.
                                                                                                        • Skin the last kill while the pet starts on the next mob.
                                                                                                        • Mend Pet to heal it, Revive Pet if it dies (Improved Revive Pet makes this reliable in combat).
                                                                                                        • Feign Death (30) drops threat on you, but mobs still go after a live pet nearby.
                                                                                                        • In dungeons, control the pet so it doesn't pull extra packs.

                                                                                                        Your Pet

                                                                                                        Taming (from Lv 10)

                                                                                                        • Every pet is Ferocity, Cunning, or Tenacity. Type has no built-in damage bonus or penalty.
                                                                                                        • Pets inherit some of your stats, so they scale with your gear.
                                                                                                        • Happiness and loyalty still exist. Feed your pet.
                                                                                                        • Each family has its own unique ability.
                                                                                                        • Leveling pet abilities: tame a higher-level beast that already knows the rank you want. Your pet won't learn higher ranks on its own. Once learned, you can teach that rank to any other pet that can use it.
                                                                                                        • Good leveling pick: a Bear. It tanks well, and Swipe (5 sec cooldown) hits several enemies, so it holds threat on groups.

                                                                                                        Gear & Stats

                                                                                                        Stat priority (Icy Veins, beta Lv30)

                                                                                                        1. Weapon damage: your #1 stat. Slower ranged weapons hit harder per shot, and Aimed Shot scales off weapon damage.
                                                                                                        2. Hit (rare on low-level gear)
                                                                                                        3. Agility: attack power and crit. Want it on every piece.
                                                                                                        4. Crit (your pet inherits it)
                                                                                                        5. Intellect (1 AP per point with Careful Aim, plus mana)
                                                                                                        6. Spirit (leveling regen only)
                                                                                                        7. Stamina
